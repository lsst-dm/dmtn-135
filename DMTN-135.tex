\setDocAbstract{%
This document gives the 2026 sizing and cost of Rubin data processing in
operations, derived from Data Preview 2 and measured Data Release Production
resource usage on the deployed hardware. The construction-era sizing model and
cost plan (last published in 2023) is retained unchanged in the appendices as a
historical record, with a table-by-table account of which of its figures are
superseded.
