\section{Introduction}\label{sec:update}

This document gives the operations sizing and cost of Rubin data processing as
of 2026, from Data Preview~2 (DP2) and the measured resource usage of Data
Release Processing (DRP) at the US Data Facility (USDF), hosted at the SLAC
Shared Scientific Data Facility (S3DF). The construction-era document is
retained unchanged in the appendices (\appref{app:construction});
\secref{sec:upstatus} gives the 2026 status of each of its tables.

\smpara{Scope.}
USDF sizing and cost are given with all processing at the USDF, and with DRP
shared with CC-IN2P3 and the UKDF. Prompt Processing runs at the USDF on
Kubernetes and is sized with the other services (\secref{sec:upservices}). The
Chilean facilities are not updated.

\smpara{Operations years.}
Tables run over operations years 1--10, FY2026--FY2035. Year~1 is DP2. In each
later year~$n$ the model sizes one processing campaign over the $n-1$ survey
years taken so far, and buys its hardware in that year. It does not schedule
data releases.

\smpara{Labels.}
Figures are \emph{measured}, \emph{estimated} from measured per-task or
per-dataset usage, or \emph{assumed}.
