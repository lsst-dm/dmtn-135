\subsection{Sizing inputs}\label{sec:upinputs}

\smpara{Processing plan.}
Each data release reprocesses every survey year to date, and there is one
release a year. That is unchanged from the plan in \secref{sec:sizeinputs}. A
release every two or three years is under discussion (\secref{sec:upunknowns}).

difference between the two sets of tables goes unexplained.
